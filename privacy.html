1. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to send

The Site does not have user accounts, a contact form, a newsletter signup, a chat tool, or a checkout. Here are the ways you can reach us or request time with us:

  • Booking a 15-minute introductory call. Each “Book a 15-minute call” button on the Site opens Google’s appointment schedule in a new window or tab, at https://calendar.app.google/rnamZXdXAbJXoZ3T8, for a session titled “IronRoot Consulting - 15-Minute Intro Call.” The booking form is hosted by Google, not by a form on our Site. Google’s booking form requires information such as your first name, last name, and email address, and may also ask for other details you choose to enter, such as a phone number, company name, or message if those fields are shown. Google provides the booking information to us so we can schedule and hold the call.
  • Email. If you write to andrew@ironrootstrategy.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.
  • Telephone. If you call (239) 341-4766, we may receive the number you call from, if it is available, and whatever you tell us on the call.
  • Mail. If you write to the address above, we receive whatever you send.

Please do not send sensitive information — for example a Social Security number, bank-account or payment-card number, or health information — through the Site, a booking form, or an unsolicited email. The Site does not ask for payment information and does not process payments.

B. Information collected automatically when you visit the Site

When you load a page on the Site, our hosting provider and the third-party services included on the page may automatically receive technical information. That can include:

  • your IP address;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (such as city, region, or country);
  • browser type, device type, language, and screen resolution;
  • the pages you view, the date and time of the visit, the referring site, and similar usage information; and
  • a client identifier stored in a first-party cookie so analytics software can distinguish visits and sessions.

These requests happen when the pages load. The Site does not currently display a cookie-consent banner or a cookie-settings panel.

C. Information we receive from service providers

Google may send us the details of appointments booked through the schedule described above, and reports about how the Site is used. We also use Google Search Console as a webmaster tool to understand how the Site appears in Google Search. Search Console provides aggregated performance information (such as queries, impressions, and clicks). It is not a form on our Site.

4. Cookies, analytics, fonts, and hosting

The Site’s own web server does not set cookies when it delivers a page. Other technologies on or linked from the Site may still collect information, including through cookies set in your browser.

Google Analytics. The Site uses Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-VJBLDFXN01, which loads as soon as a page loads. According to Google, a default Analytics implementation collects information such as number of users, session statistics, approximate geolocation, and browser and device information, and may store a client ID in first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_ followed by a container identifier (Google’s documented default duration is up to two years, though your browser may shorten that). Google may receive your IP address in order to derive approximate location. We have not independently described every setting inside our Analytics account in this policy.

Google Fonts. The Site loads fonts from Google’s servers. Google states that the Google Fonts Web API is unauthenticated and that the Google Fonts API does not set or log cookies. A font request may still include your IP address, the requested font address, and standard browser information such as user agent and referring page. Google states that it does not use information collected by Google Fonts to create profiles of end users or for targeted advertising.

Google Calendar. When you open a booking link, you use Google’s service. Google may set its own cookies on Google’s domains and collect information under Google’s privacy policy.

GitHub Pages. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub.

Your choices about cookies and analytics. You can use your browser controls to block or delete cookies. Blocking analytics cookies may limit our ability to understand how the Site is used; it does not stop you from emailing or calling us. Google also offers a browser add-on designed to opt out of Google Analytics, available at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Do Not Track and other parties. Our Site does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. Google may collect information about your use of the Site through Google Analytics and Google Fonts when pages load, and through Google Calendar if you open a booking link. We do not control whether or how Google combines that information with information from other Google services you use.
